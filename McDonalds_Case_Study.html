1. Company Profile & Key Statistics

McDonald's Corporation
Founded 1940 (franchise model since 1955)
HQ Chicago, USA
Global Stores >40,000 in 100+ countries
Australian Stores >1,000 (80% franchised)
Employees (Aus) >100,000
Revenue 2023 $25.49 billion USD
Revenue 2022 $23.18 billion USD
Business Model Franchise (~93% franchised globally)

Key Essay Statistics (2023)

Statistic Value Note
NP Ratio 33% +6pp from 2022
Current Ratio 1.16:1 Down from 1.43:1
Gearing (D/E) (869)% Negative (share buyback)
AR Turnover 32.88 days Stable
Operating Costs $13.85B Only 0.3% increase
Inventory ~$52M Stable across 2022–23

How to use these stats in an essay: "McDonald's improved its Net Profit ratio from 27% in 2022 to 33% in 2023, outpacing competitor Yum Brands (23%), demonstrating the effectiveness of its integrated marketing and cost control strategies in achieving the financial objective of profitability."

2. Operations — Strategies, Policies & Data

Supply Chain Management — Global Sourcing

Tags: Cost, Dependability, Quality

McDonald's sources ingredients from approved suppliers in multiple countries. In Australia, >90% of ingredients are locally sourced. The global supply chain uses regional distribution centres to minimise lead times.

Data point: McDonald's Australia works with >150 Australian farmers and suppliers, reducing transport costs and supporting the cost performance objective while ensuring dependability of supply.

Quality Management — TQM

Tags: Quality, Cost

McDonald's uses a Total Quality Management (TQM) approach: quality assurance systems at every stage from supplier audits to in-store cooking protocols. The "Made For You" system ensures burgers are assembled to order, reducing waste.

Data point: Standardised cooking times and temperatures across all 40,000+ stores ensure consistency — a core element of quality assurance.

Technology — Automation

Tags: Cost, Speed

Self-service kiosks installed in the majority of Australian stores. Mobile app ordering with geofencing for pickup timing. Automated inventory tracking systems link POS data to supply orders.

Data point: Kiosks reduce front-counter labour costs by ~15–20% per store while increasing average order value by 20% (upselling prompts).

Inventory Management — JIT

Tags: Cost, Quality

Just-In-Time (JIT) inventory: perishable ingredients (lettuce, buns, meat patties) delivered multiple times per week. Reduces holding costs and spoilage.

Data point: Inventory held at a stable ~$52M despite revenue growth from $23.2B to $25.5B — JIT improving efficiency.

Global Factors — Economies of Scale

Tags: Cost, Flexibility

With >40,000 stores, McDonald's achieves massive economies of scale in purchasing (bulk buying packaging, ingredients). Global R&D centres develop menu innovations that roll out across markets.

Environmental Sustainability — CSR

Tags: Customisation, Cost

Australian stores: recycled building cladding, sheep's wool insulation, solar panels. "Daisy and Liam" robots in the USA disassemble old iPhones to recover rare minerals — part of circular economy commitment.

Policy: 100% of guest packaging from renewable/recycled sources by 2025 (global target).

3. Marketing — Strategies, Policies & Data

Promotion — Integrated Campaigns

Tags: Growth, Profitability

2023 Marvel Cinematic Universe partnership: movie figurines with large meals — incentivised upsizing + repeat visits + word-of-mouth.

Data point: Revenue increased from $23,182M to $25,493M (+10%) — this promotion was a key driver alongside maintaining costs at ~$13.8B.

Pricing — Penetration + Loss Leader

Tags: Growth, Profitability

Loss Leader: $1 Frozen Coke / $1 McFlurry — brings customers in, they buy high-margin items.

Penetration: McValue menu at lower price points to capture price-sensitive consumers during a cost-of-living crisis.

Global Marketing — Glocalisation

Tags: Growth, Customisation

Standardisation: Golden Arches, "I'm Lovin' It" slogan, core menu (Big Mac, Fries) are globally consistent.

Customisation / Localisation: Australia: McCafé (premium coffee), Chicken Salt on fries, Aussie Angus Burger. India: no beef, McAloo Tikki. Japan: Teriyaki Burger.

Consumer Law Compliance (CCA 2010)

Tags: Ethics, Reputation

Previously scrutinised by the ACCC for advertising food as "healthy" despite high sugar/fat content (misleading conduct under the CCA).

Response: Introduced clear nutritional labelling on packaging and menu boards, accurate calorie counts in all advertising.

E-Marketing — Digital Ecosystem

Tags: Growth, Efficiency

MyMacca's app with loyalty program, personalised offers based on purchase history, geolocation-based promotions. >30 million app downloads in Australia.

Market Segmentation

Demographic: Families (Happy Meals), young adults (value deals, late-night), coffee drinkers (McCafé).

Behavioural: Loyalty app users get targeted deals.

Geographic: Regional menu variations.

4. Finance — Ratios, Strategies & Analysis

Financial Ratio Data (2022 vs 2023)

Ratio 2022 2023 Trend What It Means
NP Ratio (Profitability) 27% 33% Improved Revenue rose 10% while operating costs rose only ~0.3%, so profit margin expanded. Yum Brands: 23% in 2023, so McDonald's outperforms its competitor.
Current Ratio (Liquidity) 1.43:1 1.16:1 Worsened Below ideal 2:1. Still >1:1, so it can meet short-term obligations, but the declining trend is a concern.
Gearing / D/E (Solvency) (598)% (869)% Negative Negative due to an aggressive share buyback program reducing equity. Still relies more on debt than equity — high financial risk, but characteristic of mature franchise businesses with stable cash flows.
AR Turnover (Efficiency) 31.74 days 32.88 days Stable Collects receivables in ~32 days (~11.4x/year). Slight slowdown but not material for a business that is mostly cash-based.
Inventory ~$52M ~$52M Stable JIT working — revenue grew 10% but inventory didn't increase, showing efficiency gains.

Finance Strategies at McDonald's

Profitability Management — Cost Control

Tags: Profitability, Efficiency

Cost centres: Each store is treated as a cost centre with local P&L responsibility. Managers control labour scheduling, food waste, and utilities.

Data: Operating costs rose only 0.3% ($13,811.6M → $13,847M) while revenue rose 10%, demonstrating effective cost control.

Working Capital Management

Tags: Liquidity, Efficiency

Cash-based business model minimises receivables; franchisees pay royalties and rent monthly. Tight inventory via JIT keeps current assets lean. However, the declining current ratio (1.43 → 1.16) signals a need for improved working capital management.

Global Financial Management

Tags: Growth, Solvency

Hedging: McDonald's operates in 100+ countries, exposed to ~50 currencies. Uses forward contracts and currency swaps to hedge against exchange rate fluctuations, protecting AUD-denominated profits when repatriating to US HQ.

Cash Flow Management

Tags: Liquidity, Profitability

Franchise model ensures steady cash inflows (royalties ~4–5% of gross sales + rent). Discounts for early payment from franchisees. Factoring is not typically used due to mostly cash-based operations.

5. HR — Policies, Legal Compliance & Case Law

NES Compliance

McDonald's Australia complies with all 11 NES including:

  • Max 38hr/week for full-time
  • 4 weeks annual leave
  • Parental leave (12mo unpaid + 24wks paid)
  • Minimum wage $26.64/hr (above award for some roles)
  • Casual loading 25%

WHS Act 2011 Compliance

  • PPE provided: non-slip shoes, heat-resistant gloves
  • Mandatory safety training for all new hires
  • Workers compensation insurance for all employees (incl. casuals)
  • Return-to-work plans for injured workers
  • SafeWork Australia compliance audits

Enterprise Agreement

McDonald's Australia operates under an Enterprise Agreement negotiated between the company and employee representatives (SDA — Shop, Distributive and Allied Employees' Association). It must pass the BOOT (Better Off Overall Test) compared to the Fast Food Industry Award.

WFH Case Law (Fair Work Act 2009)

Key precedent: The Fair Work Commission ruled in favour of an employee's flexible working request (WFH under NES #2), allowing WFH despite employer pushback. The Commission upheld the employee's right to flexible arrangements to accommodate school drop-offs, citing strengthened workplace flexibility laws (2023 amendments to the Fair Work Act).

Source: Yun, J. (2026) "Mum wins WFH rights" — Fair Work Commission arbitration.

HR Essay Strategy: Even though HR Strategies is not assessed, HR Influences (legal framework, stakeholders, economic/tech/social) and HR Processes (acquisition, development, maintenance, separation) CAN appear in a cross-topic Section III or in the SA section. The WFH case above is an excellent contemporary example of the Fair Work Act in action for any essay discussing legal influences on business.
