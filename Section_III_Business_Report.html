📋 Section III — The Business Report

20 marks · about 35 minutes · answered in a separate writing booklet

A hypothetical business is described, followed by three or four bullet points. Each bullet is a section of your report. There is no choice — everyone answers this question. Worked examples: Trial Practice Killara 2022 · Theory: Operations | Marketing | Finance | Human Resources

1. What You Are Marked On

NESA prints the criteria above the question. All four are worth marks.

Criterion What it actually means
Demonstrate knowledge and understanding relevant to the question Correct syllabus content, defined
Apply the hypothetical business situation Quote the stimulus. Name the business. Use its numbers
Communicate using relevant business terminology and concepts Syllabus terms, not everyday words
Present a sustained, logical and cohesive response in the form of a business report Headings. Executive summary. Conclusion

"In the form of a business report" is a criterion, not a suggestion. An unheaded essay loses marks even if the content is perfect.

2. The Structure

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
  Who the business is · what the report covers · name the recommendations up front

ONE HEADED SECTION PER BULLET IN THE QUESTION
  Use the bullet's own wording as the heading

RECOMMENDATION ONE          ← if the question says "recommend TWO"
RECOMMENDATION TWO            these are TWO separate headed sections, never combined

CONCLUSION
  Restate the findings, the recommendations, and the business's stated objective

⚠️ The rule that cost marks in the Killara trial: "Recommend TWO strategies" means two separate headed paragraphs, each with two reasons. Combining them reads as one recommendation and caps the mark. Teacher's note: "Do as 2 separate paragraphs, 2 reasons for each."

The heading trick

Read the bullets. They are your headings.

Question bullet Your heading
"Describe the role of operations management for this business" THE ROLE OF OPERATIONS MANAGEMENT
"Demonstrate how legal regulation and CSR can influence operations management" INFLUENCE OF LEGAL REGULATION AND CORPORATE SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY
"Recommend TWO human resource management strategies" RECOMMENDATION ONE — [STRATEGY] and RECOMMENDATION TWO — [STRATEGY]

You cannot go off-topic if your headings are the question.

3. The Executive Summary

Four moves, about 85 words, written first and never skipped.

1. WHO      [Business] operates [what, where].
2. CONTEXT  One line on the problem from the stimulus.
3. SCOPE    This report describes… demonstrates… and recommends…
4. OBJECTIVE  …to assist in achieving [THE STATED GOAL].

From the teacher-marked Jumbo Movies report:

Jumbo Movies Pty Ltd operates a cinema complex in a large shopping centre in Western Sydney, competing in an entertainment market disrupted by streaming services and declining attendance among consumers under 30. This report describes the role of operations management for the business, demonstrates how legal regulation and corporate social responsibility influence that function, and recommends two human resource strategies, being training and development as well as rewards, to assist the new owners in achieving their objective of increasing profits by 20% within 12 months.

Naming the recommendations in the executive summary is free marks. The marker knows where you're going before they start.

4. The Body Sections

1  TOPIC      Judgement-first sentence answering the bullet.
2  DEFINE     The syllabus concept, briefly.
3  APPLY      The stimulus detail as evidence — quote it.
4  EFFECT     What that means for this business.
5  CLOSE      "Therefore / Consequently…" linking to the objective.

Every stimulus bullet must appear somewhere. The review points are not decoration — each one is a mark waiting to be claimed.

Jumbo Movies review point Where it landed
Wet, slippery floors WHS duty of care breach
Run down environment and uniforms High visibility (4 Vs) + non-monetary rewards
Coffee machine took 6 months Dependability failure
Ticketing machine errors Quality failure + training
Working through lunch breaks Fair Work / NES + non-monetary rewards
Unhappy with training Training and development

Before you write, tick each stimulus bullet off against a section. Any bullet with no home is a mark you're about to leave behind.

5. The Recommendation Sections

One heading each. Two reasons each. Name the syllabus category.

1  NAME      It is recommended that [BUSINESS] implement [STRATEGY],
             a [FUNCTION] strategy within [SYLLABUS CATEGORY].
2  REASON 1  The first reason is [X]. [STIMULUS EVIDENCE].
3  REASON 2  The second reason is [Y]. [EFFECT + INDICATOR].
4  LINK      …contributing directly to [THE STATED OBJECTIVE].

Worked, from the corrected Jumbo Movies report:

RECOMMENDATION ONE — TRAINING AND DEVELOPMENT

It is recommended that Jumbo Movies implement a structured training and development program, a human resource strategy within the development stage of the HR cycle. The first reason is that it directly addresses the errors identified in the review. Employees are dissatisfied with the training received on the new ticketing machines and regular errors are occurring, indicating the technology was introduced without adequate skill development, so on the job training combined with customer service training would reduce these errors and improve the performance objectives of quality and speed. The second reason is retention. Employees who are supported to develop are more motivated and less likely to leave, reducing staff turnover, a key indicator of human resource effectiveness, which lowers recruitment and induction costs and contributes directly to the 20% profit objective.

6. Worked Example — Your Full-Marks Apple Report

This is the structure that earned full marks on your Finance assessment. Same shape, different subject. Full text: Brain/Business_Studies/Past_Essays/Finance_Assessment_Draft.md

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
  Apple Inc. is a large global technology company that designs, manufactures
  and sells consumer electronics, software and digital services worldwide.
  This report assesses Apple's financial performance over the 2024 and 2025
  financial years using the net profit ratio, expense ratio, and accounts
  receivable turnover (days). It also recommends the use of factoring…

PROFITABILITY — NET PROFIT RATIO          ← one heading per ratio
EFFICIENCY — EXPENSE RATIO
EFFICIENCY OF COLLECTION — ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE TURNOVER
RECOMMENDATION — IMPROVE ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE TURNOVER
CONCLUSION

Why each body section scored:

1  JUDGEMENT   "Apple's profitability improved between 2024 (23.97%) and
               2025 (26.92%), as shown by the net profit ratio, which
               measures how much profit is retained after expenses."
2  DATA        Both years' raw figures and the calculation.
3  MEANING     "…meaning Apple retained approximately 24 cents for every
               $1 of revenue."
4  CAUSE       "…driven by growth in services revenue, which increased
               from $96,169m to $109,158m."
5  BENCHMARK   "Compared to Samsung, which operates with a net profit
               ratio of approximately 10%…"
6  IMPLICATION "…supports reinvestment, expansion, and long term growth."

The move that made it full marks: a benchmark in every section. Samsung for profitability, Samsung again for the expense ratio, the 30-day ideal for receivables. Never a number without a comparison.

7. Timing and Length

Phase Time
Read the stimulus, tick each bullet to a section 3 min
Executive summary 4 min
Body sections 20 min
Recommendations 6 min
Conclusion 2 min

750–800 words, roughly 20 words a minute handwritten.

If you're running out of time, cut examples and detail from the body sections. Never cut the recommendations or the conclusion — they carry their own marks and the "sustained and cohesive" criterion.

8. Checklist

□ Executive summary present, names the recommendations
□ One heading per question bullet, using the bullet's wording
□ Recommendations in SEPARATE headed sections, two reasons each
□ Every stimulus detail used somewhere
□ Business named repeatedly, not "the business"
□ Syllabus terminology throughout
□ Every judgement has a benchmark or comparison
□ Conclusion restates findings + recommendations + the stated objective

The four most common ways to lose marks here:

  1. No headings. Loses the "form of a business report" criterion outright.
  2. Generic content. Theory that could describe any business. Quote the stimulus.
  3. Combining two recommendations. Two means two sections.
  4. Ignoring the stated objective. If the question says "increase profits by 20% within 12 months", every recommendation must link back to it.
