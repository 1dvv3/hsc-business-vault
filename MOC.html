The three placements that catch people

Where it feels like it goes Where the syllabus puts it
Monitoring, control and improvement Strategies Operations PROCESSES — because "quality management: control, assurance, improvement" is separately a strategy, the same words appear twice
Market segmentation and positioning The marketing process (next to target markets) Marketing STRATEGIES — only identifying target markets is a process step
Stakeholders Role of HRM HR INFLUENCES — it's the first dot point there

Quick Formulas — Finance

Full worked examples in Finance 3 Processes §5.

LIQUIDITY
  Current Ratio        = Current Assets / Current Liabilities        ideal 2:1

SOLVENCY
  Gearing Ratio        = Total Liabilities / Owners' Equity          ideal 0.5:1 to 1:1

PROFITABILITY
  Gross Profit Ratio   = (Gross Profit / Sales) x 100                higher is better
  Net Profit Ratio     = (Net Profit / Sales) x 100                  higher is better
  Return on Owners'    = (Net Profit / Owners' Equity) x 100         higher is better
    Equity

EFFICIENCY
  Expense Ratio        = (Total Expenses / Sales) x 100              lowest possible
  Accounts Receivable  = Step 1: Sales / Accounts Receivable
    Turnover             Step 2: 365 / Step 1 answer                 ideal 30 days

BREAK-EVEN
  Break-Even (units)   = Fixed Costs / (Selling Price - Variable Cost)
  Contribution         = Selling Price - Variable Cost per unit
  Margin of Safety     = Actual sales - Break-even sales

The Three Financial Statements

CASH FLOW STATEMENT
  Opening Cash Balance + Cash Receipts - Cash Payments = Closing Cash Balance

INCOME STATEMENT
  Sales
  Less Sales Returns
  = NET SALES
  Less COGS  (Opening Stock + Purchases - Closing Stock)
  = GROSS PROFIT
  Less Expenses  (selling + administrative + financial)
  = NET PROFIT

  Check:  Gross Profit Ratio - Expense Ratio = Net Profit Ratio

BALANCE SHEET
  Owners' Equity = Assets (Current + Non-Current) - Liabilities (Current + Non-Current)

Exam Tips

  1. Always apply theory to a case study — Apple, McDonald's, BYD.
  2. Use specific data — ratios, figures, percentages, dates.
  3. Show interdependence between the four functions.
  4. Interpret, don't just calculate — a ratio means nothing without a judgement on it.
  5. Link back to the strategic role — cost leadership/differentiation (Ops), profit maximisation (Marketing), the five objectives (Finance), effectiveness indicators (HR).
  6. Know your verbs and match answer length to marks.
