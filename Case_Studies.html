How to Use Case Studies in the HSC

The HSC exam requires you to apply theory to real businesses. Every extended response and most short answer questions need case study references.

Case study framework:

  1. Background — industry, size, location, products/services
  2. Operations — how they produce/deliver, strategies used
  3. Marketing — target market, marketing mix, positioning
  4. Finance — performance (ratios), funding, budgets
  5. HR — staffing, training, culture
  6. Recommendations — based on analysis, justified with theory

Apple Inc. — Finance Case Study

Background

  • Industry: Consumer electronics, software, services
  • Size: One of the world's largest companies by market cap
  • Products: iPhone, Mac, iPad, Wearables, Services (Apple Music, iCloud, App Store)
  • Global presence: Americas, Europe, Greater China, Japan, Rest of Asia Pacific

Financial Data (2024-2025)

Income Statement Highlights:

Item Sept 2025 Sept 2024 Change
Products revenue $307,003M $294,866M +4.1%
Services revenue $109,158M $96,169M +13.5%
Total revenue $416,161M $391,035M +6.4%
Net income $112,010M $93,736M +19.5%

Revenue by segment (2025): Americas 42.9%, Europe 26.7%, Greater China 15.5%, Japan 6.9%, Rest of Asia Pacific 8.1%

Revenue by product (2025): iPhone 50.4%, Services 26.2%, Wearables 8.6%, Mac 8.1%, iPad 6.7%

Financial Ratios (Apple)

Ratio 2025 2024 Trend
Current Ratio 0.89:1 0.87:1 Slightly improved but below ideal 2:1
Net Profit Margin 26.9% 24.0% Improved
Gearing (D/E) 3.87:1 5.41:1 Improved — less debt reliance
AR Turnover 34.9 days 31.2 days Slightly worsened

Key Takeaways

Strengths: Strong NPM improvement (24% → 26.9%), revenue growth across all segments, services growing fastest (+13.5%), gearing improved significantly, low inventory levels

Weaknesses: Current ratio well below 2:1, AR turnover worsened, heavy iPhone reliance (50% of revenue), China market risk

How to use in exam: "For example, Apple's net profit margin improved from 24.0% to 26.9%, demonstrating that their revenue growth strategies have been effective. However, their current ratio of 0.89:1 remains below the ideal 2:1, indicating potential liquidity concerns."

McDonald's Corporation — Finance Case Study

Background

  • Industry: Fast food / Quick Service Restaurant (QSR)
  • Size: Global — 40,000+ restaurants in 100+ countries
  • Business model: Primarily franchise-based (95% franchised)

Financial Ratios (Historical)

Ratio 2022 2021 2020 2019
Current Ratio 1.43:1 1.78:1 1.01:1 0.98:1
Net Profit Margin 26.65% 32.49% 24.63% 28.20%
Gross Profit Margin 56.97% 54.17% 50.77% 52.32%

McDonald's 2022-2023 Analysis

Ratio 2022 2023 Analysis
Current Ratio 1.43:1 1.16:1 Worsened — increase in short-term borrowing
Gearing (D/E) -598% -869% Negative due to share buyback
Net Profit Margin 27% 33% Improved — increased sales while controlling costs
AR Turnover 31.74 days 32.88 days Slightly worsened but close to ideal 30 days

Example HSC Response

Recommendation: "It is recommended for McDonald's to use cost centres to improve their profitability."

Why: Cost centres minimise costs by allowing McDonald's to allocate budgets and responsibilities to each centre to find ways to minimise costs such as buying raw materials locally rather than controlling from head office in America.

Marketing link: McDonald's implemented a promotional campaign in 2023 giving away Marvel movie figures with large meals, incentivising more customers and increasing sales from $23,182M to $25,493M.

Perfect Fresh Pty Ltd — Operations & Marketing Case Study

Background

  • Industry: Food manufacturing, wholesale supply
  • Product: Fresh salads for caterers and wholesale outlets
  • Market: Sydney region
  • Key innovation: Plastic storage bag extending salad shelf life

Problems Identified

  1. Late deliveries to customers
  2. Poor product presentation (packaging)
  3. Low productivity and high staff absenteeism

Analysis by Business Function

Operations — Inputs (Packaging):

  • Packaging is a critical input directly influencing freshness and visual appeal
  • Poor-quality packaging → damage, reduced freshness, poor presentation
  • Solution: High-quality, durable, visually appealing packaging

Operations — Transformation (Technology):

  • Modern equipment improves speed, consistency, hygiene
  • Produces uniform, professional look
  • Solution: Upgrade technology to improve productivity, reduce errors

Marketing — Product Strategy:

  • Premium, branded packaging strategy
  • Creates strong visual identity, stands out in wholesale settings
  • Reinforces commitment to quality and freshness

HR — Staffing Issues:

  • Low productivity and high absenteeism suggest poor motivation
  • Link to motivation theories: Herzberg — ensure hygiene factors (working conditions, job security) are met, then use motivators (recognition, responsibility)

Tips for Using Case Studies in the Exam

Short Answer Questions

  • Always name the business
  • Link theory directly to the case study
  • Use specific data/figures when available
  • "For example, Apple's net profit margin improved from 24.0% to 26.9%..."

Extended Response / Business Report

  • Reference the case study in every section
  • Use ratios and financial data to support arguments
  • Compare across years or against industry benchmarks
  • Make specific, justified recommendations

Key Formula

[Theory/Concept] + [Case Study Evidence] + [Link to Question] = Full Marks

Exam trap: Don't just describe the case study — you must link it to the theory and answer the specific question asked.
